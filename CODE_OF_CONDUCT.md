# Code of Conduct

*(Structure is final and in force; the details reflect a small, focused project.
Update this file if the project's outbound role changes — see `GOVERNANCE.md`.)*

## Our standards

Cadence is a small, focused project built for one professional audience.
Participation is expected to be direct, technical and respectful.

**Do:**

- Assume competence and good faith; state disagreements as technical points.
- Keep criticism about code and documents, never about people.
- Give, and accept, precise feedback — including the exact failing case.
- Link the facts when reopening a closed discussion.
- Welcome people learning scheduling, Rust, or both.

**Do not:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or hostile commentary of any kind.
- Spam, or unrelated promotion of the contributor's own work.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Demanding that maintainers work to an external deadline.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every project space: the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, and release drafts. A maintainer may consider
behaviour outside those spaces when it damages the community's ability to do
this work, but only when that behaviour is directly connected to the project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards, and take appropriate,
proportionate action in response to behaviour they judge inappropriate,
threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues or other contributions that do not follow this code
of conduct, and will give reasons when the circumstances allow it.

## Reporting

Report violations to the maintainers at the contact in `README.md`, or through
GitHub's private reporting tools. Reports are confidential to the people
handling them. Every report gets a response within seven days, even when the
response is only that it is being looked at. Reports concerning a maintainer are
handled by someone else — say so in the report and that is automatic.

## Enforcement actions

Consequences are graduated:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the violation and what changes.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning, recorded internally, with stated
   consequences if the behaviour continues.
3. **Temporary ban** — time-limited ban from interaction in project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — for continued violations, or a single severe one.

Maintainers may also use narrower measures where they fit: closing a thread,
locking it, or removing a single comment or file. The goal is to protect people
who work on and use Cadence and to keep the project usable, not to punish.
